Notes: this mockup is for workflow review, not a pixel-perfect copy of the current firmware screens. It intentionally reflects the cleaner HTML-style guided process we discussed: one target pad at a time, repeated normal hits, one hard hit, detect bleed, raise only offending pads, then review and save.
