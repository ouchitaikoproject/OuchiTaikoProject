Smarter. Simpler. Better.

An open-source Arcade-Scale home Taiko drum project with step-by-step build guide, project files, firmware, and fool-proof guided calibration.

In Japanese, "ouchi" means "home," and "taiko" means "drum." Together, OuchiTaiko brings the arcade Taiko experience into your own space.
