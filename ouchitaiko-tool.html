How to use: Switch your drum out of Calibrate mode into any gamepad mode (Switch Tatacon, PS4, XInput, etc.).
Plug in via USB — it appears as a gamepad. Press any button and it lights up above matching your actual PCB.

Drum hits: Ka pads = ZL/ZR · Don pads = L3/R3 in Switch Tatacon mode · Face: X=North B=South Y=West A=East
